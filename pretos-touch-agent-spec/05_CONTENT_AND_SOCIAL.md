# Pretos Touch — Content & Social Strategy

## Core content pillars

### 1. Education
Teach customers how to choose/use products.

### 2. Product demonstration
Show products in real use.

### 3. Customer confidence
Show comfort, convenience and everyday lifestyle benefits.

### 4. Mother/baby care
Practical content for new parents.

### 5. Social proof
Reviews, UGC, testimonials and creator content.

## Social-to-site funnel

```text
TikTok / Instagram / Facebook
        ↓
Short-form content
        ↓
Product/category landing page
        ↓
Education + proof + product details
        ↓
Add to cart / WhatsApp
        ↓
Purchase
        ↓
Review / UGC
        ↓
More content
```

## Social landing pages

The architecture should support campaign landing pages such as:

`/collections/tiktok`

`/collections/instagram`

or campaign-specific pages.

These pages should be optimized for conversion and may be noindex if they are duplicate/temporary campaign pages.

## Blog-to-social workflow

One article should produce multiple social assets.

Example:

Article:
`How to Use a Postpartum Belt`

Repurpose into:
- 30-second Reel
- 5-slide Instagram carousel
- Facebook post
- Story FAQ
- Product demonstration
- WhatsApp broadcast content where appropriate

## Content voice

Pretos Touch should sound:
- Helpful
- Warm
- Confident
- Simple
- Human

Avoid:
- Corporate jargon
- Fear-based marketing
- Shame-based messaging
- Medical certainty without evidence
- Excessive exclamation marks

## Calls to action

## UGC system

Build components/placeholders for:
- Customer photo
- Customer quote
- Product rating
